# Dream and Not Dream: English style rules

Read this page and glossary.json before you start. Twenty people are translating in parallel, so consistency matters more than any single good phrase.

## Voice
1. Write plain, short sentences. Use the most ordinary word that is accurate. No literary flourish, no added explanation, no intensifiers the Chinese does not have. American spelling.
2. The player is "you". Narration stays in the tense the Chinese implies, usually past. Do not switch to "Wang Hongwen" where the Chinese says 你.
3. Keep the spare, reported feel of Chinese dialogue. 他说，要开，先投票。 becomes He said, if we open it, we vote first.

## Banned forms (the author's rules, checked by script)
4. No em dashes and no en dashes. Use a comma, a period or parentheses. Hyphens inside words (Shanghai-Nanjing, 4-3 Program) are fine.
5. No colon that introduces a list. Write a full sentence instead.
6. No "not X but Y", "rather than", "instead of X, Y" contrasts. State the positive claim on its own.
7. No exclamation marks, even where the Chinese has one. A slogan ending in ！ ends with a period or nothing.

## Terms and names
8. glossary.json is binding, capitalization included. Plurals are fine. If you need a term that is missing, pick a plain rendering and report it so it can be added.
9. Names are pinyin, family name first, no hyphen or tone marks (Wang Hongwen, Zhang Chunqiao, Zhou Yue'e). Fictional people follow the same rule. Never add an explanation at first mention; the game has pop-up entries.
10. Glossary values in Title Case are labels (stats, factions, world types, endings, cards, dimensions). Use the Title Case form only when the string is the label itself. In running prose use ordinary lowercase English (the veteran cadres, the army, an air-raid shelter).
11. 工总司 is Workers' General Headquarters at the first mention in a scene or note, then WGHQ. 中央文革 is Central Cultural Revolution Group, then CCRG. 革委会 is lowercase as a generic noun and capitalized in a proper name (Shanghai Revolutionary Committee).
12. 一月风暴 is January Storm, the event. 一月革命 is January Revolution, used only where the text means the official name of the time.

## Recurring problems
13. 主席. 毛主席 is Chairman Mao. A bare 主席 meaning Mao is the Chairman. 华主席 is Chairman Hua. 主席台 is the rostrum. For union or committee heads use lowercase chairman.
14. 同志. Keep "Comrade" in direct address and in documents (Comrade Chunqiao, Comrade Hongwen). Drop it in narration when it is only politeness. 同志们 in a speech is comrades.
15. 师傅 before a name is Master (Master Qian). 小王, 老陆 are Little Wang, Old Lu.
16. Slogans, quotations from Mao and document titles are translated literally and kept short, in quotes the first time (“grasp revolution, promote production”). Use the glossary wording every time; readers learn them by repetition.
17. Official jargon stays official in English when a character speaks it or a document says it. When the narrator says the same thing, use plain words.
18. 造反派 is rebels, 保守派 conservatives, 保皇派 royalists (an insult). Keep each side's own word for itself in their dialogue.

## Format
19. Dates. 一九六六年十一月 is November 1966; 1966年11月10日 is November 10, 1966. Years, dates, money and counts above ten are digits. 一千多万 is more than ten million.
20. Quotation marks are curly. Use “ ” for quotes and slogans, ‘ ’ inside them. The apostrophe is the plain ' (Workers').
21. Use the glossary's English title for books, papers and documents, and add no italics tags. In source notes write Author, English title, then the Chinese title in 《》 in parentheses, e.g. Huang Jinhai, Ten Years Not a Dream (《十年非梦》), ch. 9 (paraphrased).
22. Keep every HTML tag, attribute and entity exactly. Translate only visible text and the values of alt, title and aria-label.
23. Keep every placeholder such as ⟦0⟧ exactly once. You may move it where English grammar needs it. Assume it can be empty.
24. Keep leading and trailing spaces and the full-width space 　 where they are.
25. Source tags are History, Disputed, Fiction and Reconstructed from sources. 转述 is (paraphrased), 搜索摘要 is (search summary).
